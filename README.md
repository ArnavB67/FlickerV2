In Flicker you are trapped in a Dark environment, your goal is to locate the electric box and restore power by filling 3 fully charged batteries, you can find batteries scattered randomly across the map. However your torch and camera need battery to operate so you need to work on power management to win.

As a player you get an inventory which you can open by pressing tab, you can drag and drop batteries from one slot to another including torch and camera the 2 items you get,
with torch you can control its brightness to decide his field of vision but you cant see the ghost
with a camera you can control your fov but you can see ghost with it, however camera uses more battery than a torch so you have to manage.
If you hear heartbeat sounds it means ghost has started to notice you, so you have 2 seconds to run away or decrease your torch light till the heartbeat sounds stop, however if you exceed 2 seconds the ghost will chase you and its game over
you can search stuff in cabinets by pressing E and open inventory by pressing tab
